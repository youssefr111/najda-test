\bottomrule\endlastfoot
\textbf{AI priority} & A model-assigned urgency (critical, high, medium, low) shown to dispatchers as decision support; it never changes what a dispatcher may do.\\
\textbf{Caller trust} & A score from a caller’s recent false reports and late cancellations that can slow, but never refuse, new reports.\\
\textbf{Cool-down} & A 10-minute minimum gap between reports applied to callers in the COOLDOWN or REVIEW tier.\\
\textbf{Crew /\allowbreak{} LEAD} & Roles on a shift. The LEAD is the unit’s single accountable operator.\\
\textbf{Custom claim} & A role value stored in the Firebase ID token for convenience; the database remains authoritative.\\
\textbf{Didit} & The identity-verification provider used for the document-and-selfie check.\\
\textbf{Facility} & A hospital, ambulance station, fire station or police station.\\
\textbf{Handoff} & The ambulance workflow from arrival on scene to delivering the patient at a hospital.\\
\textbf{Idempotent} & Safe to repeat: the same request id always yields the same incident.\\
\textbf{KYC} & Know your customer --- identity verification of a person.\\
\textbf{Mission} & One unit’s assignment to one incident.\\
\textbf{Outbox} & The mobile app’s local queue of reports waiting to be sent.\\
\textbf{Registered facility} & A facility with active staff or a non-archived unit, usable as a station or hospital destination.\\
\textbf{Response unit} & An ambulance, fire truck, police car or a first responder’s personal unit.\\
\textbf{Signed URL} & A short-lived link that lets a client upload or download one object directly from storage.\\
\textbf{STOMP} & A simple messaging protocol carried over WebSocket and used for live updates.\\
\textbf{Vitals} & Measured vital signs: heart rate, SpO\textsubscript{2}, blood pressure, respiratory rate, temperature, consciousness level.\\
